Loading the coop…

A Linux ISO mirror watched for ten days. Throughput is the public port, averaged per 5 minutes and per hour, in Gbps. A chicken is one ISO the mirror picked up; counts use the time it was added. Days are numbered from the first day of the log, hours are UTC. Day 1 starts at 10:30 and the last day is partial.

Each point is the average of one hour. The faint line is the busiest 5-minute bucket inside that hour, so you can see how spiky the hour was.
